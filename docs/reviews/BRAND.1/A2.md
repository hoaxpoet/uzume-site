# BRAND.1 — A.2 myth-resolution brief

Direction A and Alumni Sans are fixed for this increment. A.2 does not reopen the typography or the overall identity lane; it tests how much of the Ama-no-Iwato story the mark can carry without literal imagery.

## Fixed system

- Display and wordmark basis: Alumni Sans, controlled medium-to-semibold weights.
- Text and product UI: PT Sans.
- Color strategy: near-black, warm neutral text, and one first-light amber accent. Footage remains the only full-color field.
- Shape language: measured vertical events, softened ends, no religious or figurative symbols.

## Mark evolutions

### Aperture

Four measured events flank one narrow amber opening. The central event has transformed from a beat into a threshold: the planned performance has opened the dark room to first light. This is the strongest mythic reading and the least literal; its risk is that the narrow central stroke may need optical adjustment at favicon sizes.

### Arrival

The original five-event Measure mark remains intact, but the fourth event—the longest and latest gesture—carries first light. This preserves the strongest continuity with A and reads immediately at small sizes. Its risk is also the known risk: without surrounding language it remains closest to an equalizer.

### Reflection

Five events are arranged across a quiet horizontal divide, with their lower halves returning as restrained reflections and the center catching amber. This joins planned rhythm to the mirror moment in the myth. It carries the most narrative information, but is the most complex at 16 px.
