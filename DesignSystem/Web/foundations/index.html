What belongs here

Foundations / Accessibility

Accessibility

Accessibility is a component contract, not a review-stage patch.

  • Normal text meets 4.5:1; meaningful boundaries and graphics meet 3:1.
  • Disabled controls retain 4.5:1 by Uzume policy and include an associated reason.
  • Status uses color, icon, and explicit language in both appearances.
  • Interactive targets are at least 44 × 44 px and expose visible keyboard focus.
  • Reduced motion, increased contrast, long labels, and media failure are defined states.
