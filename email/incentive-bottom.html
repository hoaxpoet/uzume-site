Uzume is a Mac app that listens to whatever music you're already playing and performs light to it. It reads your playlist first, plans what to show, then adjusts as the music goes.
Analysis and rendering run locally. Uzume has no account, sends no audio anywhere, and needs no network connection to perform. It will be free and open source, MIT.
