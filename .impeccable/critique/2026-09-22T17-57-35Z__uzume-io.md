## Design Specificity Verdict

Homepage: authored at both ends, template in the middle third (auto-fit feature grid, stacked status callouts, poster-card grid). Product-authored: the letterboxed hero band with the glyph-halo scrim instead of a veil; the scroll-driven lockup handoff on a view-timeline anchored to the h1; the three-beat mechanism copy.

Gallery: strongly authored and the better surface. One performance per row; captions that map audio feature to visual parameter on silent clips. Category-default element: bare `<video controls>` — the browser's own control bar is the most generic thing on the site.

Deterministic scan: `impeccable detect` silently skips `.astro` (proven with a byte-identical control: exit 2 as .html, exit 0 as .astro). Live-URL scans: homepage exit 2, 6 findings (tight-leading x1, ai-color-palette x4, gray-on-color x1); gallery exit 2, 8 findings (heading-rhythm x8) plus an em-dash advisory. Contrast gate exit 0, 60 pairings.

False positives: ai-color-palette reports "cyan neon" — there is no cyan, the accent is #7f6aff violet. gray-on-color measures 9.32:1. tight-leading fires on display type where sub-1.3 leading is correct. heading-rhythm x8 is genuine.

Visual overlays: none. Injection blocked by the site's own `script-src 'self'` CSP — the site working correctly.

## What's Working

1. The hero's glyph halo instead of a veil — three text-shadow rings in --color-canvas sized in em, so the sky keeps its light everywhere but the letterforms.
2. The gallery captions — eight sentences translating an audio feature into a visual parameter, on deliberately silent clips.
3. Reduced motion handled through three independent mechanisms: the observer never registers under reduce, the scroll-driven header animations are declared inside a no-preference query, and the hero opening uses `backwards` fill.
