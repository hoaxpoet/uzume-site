## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The chrome is authored with real care; the *page* is category-interchangeable. Genuinely Uzume's and unliftable: `uzume-icon.png` (the prismatic bloom — the only place the opening spectrum exists on the whole site, at 128 CSS px), Alumni Sans as a display face, the Coleridge and Kojiki passages, and the copy voice. Interchangeable: the centered-column hero, the ambient violet radial glow (`index.astro:181-194`) — which `DESIGN.md:208` explicitly forbids — the three Bootstrap-semantic banners, the 3-up text grid, the bordered contributor card.

The structural indictment: `BRAND.md`'s thesis is that the engine's output *is* the brand, with chrome receding so footage can lead. The chrome recedes on schedule. **The footage never arrives.** `DESIGN.md:202` requires real performance footage in the first viewport; there is none anywhere. `VideoTile.astro` and `PresetCard.astro` are built, work, and appear only on `/design`. The restraint shipped without the thing it was restraining for, so what's visible is the template underneath.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json src/pages src/components src/layouts` → **exit 0, zero findings**. Confirmed a real result, not a no-op: `.astro` is in `SCANNABLE_EXTENSIONS`, so all 11 files parsed. The detector agreed with nothing and caught nothing — every issue below came from human-grade review or direct DOM measurement. Not scanned (outside scope): `src/styles/*.css`, `src/content/**.md`, `tokens.css`.

**Visual overlays: unavailable.** Injection was attempted properly and failed. Mutation preflight *passed* (`document.title` set and restored; an appended `<script>` executed). Production CSP in `public/_headers` blocks both paths: `script-src` rejects `<script src="http://localhost:8400/detect.js">`, and `connect-src` rejects the `fetch()` fallback. `'unsafe-inline'` alone does not rescue it. **No user-visible overlay exists in any browser tab.** Fallback signal: direct DOM/network measurement, reported below.

## Overall Impression

This site is unusually honest and unusually invisible. The beta-honesty apparatus is the best-executed thing here and better than most shipping products manage. The engineering is clean: zero framework JS, 154KB total, contrast gate passing on all 60 pairings, reduced motion honored globally and correctly.

And it never once shows you the thing. It is a visualizer's website with no visuals. The single biggest opportunity is not a redesign — it is one 8-second clip in the first viewport. Everything that reads as generic reads that way because the space the chrome politely vacated was never filled.

## What's Working

1. **Beta honesty goes all the way into the affordance.** The button says "On GitHub today — build from source" (`index.astro:71`) — the constraint lives inside the label, so it cannot be skimmed past. Paired with the double-opt-in status: *"Almost — check your email and confirm. Nothing happens until you do."* Most products write "You're in! 🎉" there and silently lose half the list.
2. **The trust band answers objections before they form.** Three callouts in the order a Mac user actually worries — privacy → permission → photosensitivity. The permission copy names the macOS quirk rather than defending itself. Flash safety is D-157-accurate throughout: bounded per-frame brightness change, beat-locked motion confined to regions, **no flashes-per-second figure anywhere**. Fully compliant.
3. **Token and accessibility discipline are real, not claimed.** `check_contrast.py` passes 60 pairings (tightest: info border 3.05:1 against a 3.0 floor). Zero images without alt, zero non-descriptive link text, zero horizontal overflow at 375px (`scrollWidth 375 === innerWidth 375`), zero console errors, zero 4xx/5xx. Reduced motion covered three ways: a global `*` reset (`Base.astro:81`), token collapse to 1ms (`tokens.css:243`), and a `matchMedia` gate on video (`VideoTile.astro:62`).

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Nothing on the site shows the engine
**Why it matters:** This is a visualizer. A listener cannot want it without seeing it; a contributor cannot judge the visual bar without seeing the repertoire. It is also *why* the page reads as generic. Measured: zero requests to `media.uzume.io` on any page. The largest color fields on the live page are a blue info banner and a green success banner.
**Fix:** Ship one loop in the first viewport — a single 8s H.264 of a certified preset, poster-first, `preload="none"`, autoplay gated on reduced-motion. `VideoTile.astro` already does all of this. **Check this first:** the CSP is `img-src 'self'` with **no `media-src` directive**, so `default-src 'self'` will silently block every asset from `media.uzume.io`. Both agents flagged this independently. Add `media-src https://media.uzume.io` and extend `img-src` in the same commit, and verify in a browser — curl will not catch it, which is the exact failure class the file's own comment documents.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable bolder`

### [P0] `/docs` discards the brand one click from the homepage
**Why it matters:** "Darkness is the condition" is the creative premise. `/docs` is in the global nav on every page and serves `data-theme="light"` (canvas `#f7f7f5`), accent `#6753d7` instead of `#7f6aff`, and `document.fonts` reports `Alumni Sans: unloaded`. `Base.astro:21` hardcodes dark; Starlight's root follows OS preference and `src/styles/docs.css` never pins it. The one page a contributor visits to assess seriousness is the one page that looks unowned — and its content is "Documentation is not written yet."
**Fix:** Pin `data-theme="dark"` on `<html>` via the Starlight `head` config (next to the beacon at `astro.config.mjs:16-25`), drop `--sl-theme-select`, map `--sl-font-headings: var(--font-display)`, and add the Alumni Sans `@font-face` to the docs `customCss` chain. Until W.5 fills it, consider pointing the nav link at the repo instead of a dead end.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P1] The hero defers its two most decision-relevant facts past the fold, and offers two primary CTAs
**Why it matters:** Persuade mode. Hero height is 961px at 1024×768 and 1,027px at 375×812 — below the fold on both: the email field *and* the "no installable build yet" note, which is the page's lowest-emphasis type (14px `#a4a8a2`, the smallest and faintest on the page). Above the fold: a violet button leading to a repo. Both hero actions are the same `#7f6aff` fill with opposite intents — act now vs. defer. An Intel Mac owner invests a full scroll before discovering they were never eligible.
**Fix:** Move the availability line *above* the CTA row and raise it to `--text-base`/secondary; compress the Coleridge quote to caption scale so the hero fits ~620px. Append "Apple silicon · macOS 14+ · free, MIT" under the buttons. Demote build-from-source to `variant="secondary"` — it is the narrow audience — and let the notify field carry the primary fill.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] The site publishes the wrong pronunciation of its own name
**Why it matters:** `index.astro:137` renders **"oo-ZOO-may"** in the one section whose entire purpose is teaching the name. Four canonical sources say **oo-ZOO-meh**: `PRODUCT.md:39`, `BRAND.md:11`, `CLAUDE.md:3`, `docs/planning/NAMING_REPORT.md:7` ("three open syllables"). A comment at `index.astro:304` rationalizes the capitalization but not the vowel, so this reads as a drafting slip that hardened. This is a source-of-truth-boundary breach, not a style choice.
**Fix:** Change to `oo-ZOO-meh` at `index.astro:137` and update the comment at `:304`.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] No `<h1>`, broken heading ladder, no skip link, no social card
**Why it matters:** Verified live: **`h1Count: 0`** on the homepage; outline is `H2 ×5 → H3 → H2`, with "Contributors write the repertoire" (`index.astro:123`) an `H3` with no parent `H2`. `curl` returns **no `og:` or `twitter:` tags at all** — `Base.astro:20-32` emits none, and grep for `og:|twitter:` across `src/` returns nothing. `/docs` has them only because Starlight generates its own. The target persona *arrived from a shared link* — every share into iMessage, Slack or Discord renders as a bare `uzume.io` string. The icon, the best asset the brand owns, is the one thing that never travels. Screen-reader users land with no announced page title and an outline they cannot navigate by level.
**Fix:** Wrap the wordmark image in an `<h1>` (keep `alt="Uzume"`), promote the contributor heading to `H2`, add a visually-hidden skip link to `Base.astro`, and add `og:title` / `og:description` / `og:image` / `twitter:card="summary_large_image"` with a self-hosted 1200×630 card built from the icon on `--color-canvas` (`img-src 'self'` requires self-hosting — it already would be).
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] The 404 is an empty document
**Why it matters:** Verified: `status=404 bytes=0`. No markup, no title, no background, no link home. `astro.config.mjs:29` sets `disable404Route: true` and `src/pages/404.astro` does not exist. Every stale or mistyped link — including `/gallery` and `/download`, which `Nav.astro:6-7` names as forthcoming and which people will guess — terminates in a blank white screen on a site whose identity is black.
**Fix:** Add `src/pages/404.astro` on `Base.astro`: wordmark, one sentence, one link home, one to GitHub. Twenty lines.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## Emotional Journey

Modest rise → one real peak → a long well-mannered plateau → a quiet actionless fade.

The peak is the first 400px: the icon over black with the violet bloom, then the wordmark in Alumni Sans. Stronger on mobile than desktop. Immediately after comes a valley — a 231-year-old poem is the biggest thing on screen and the visitor still doesn't know what the product is.

The high-stakes moment — *"can I have it?"* — is answered honestly and positioned badly. `index.astro:75-77` is exactly right and is below the fold on a 13" laptop *and* on iPhone, in the page's faintest style. The site tells the truth in a whisper, positioned behind the thing it's correcting. The disappointment is disclosed *after* the click, not before.

Genuine reassurance arrives at ~1,750px with the trust callouts — the emotional high point of the body.

**Peak-end is violated.** The page ends on a Kojiki paragraph; the last action offered is "Write a preset," which is for a different persona. The most engaged visitor you will ever get reaches the bottom and the page has nothing to say to them. No second notify prompt, no "so what now." A full stop where there should be a door.

## Minor Observations

- **PT Sans never loads.** `tokens.css:84` declares it; `src/styles/fonts.css` ships only Alumni Sans (the comment at 9-11 explains: a 2.7MB `.ttc`). All body copy renders in `system-ui`. Half the type system is aspirational.
- **Alumni Sans ships as a 133KB `.ttf`, not `.woff2`** (`fonts.css:14`) — roughly 2× the transfer it needs, on the critical path. With `uzume-icon.png` (69.5KB PNG at 256×256) that is 93% of page weight.
- **Two different `H2` treatments at the same level** — `.beat h2` is 22px in the body face; `.band > h2` is 40px Alumni Sans.
- **Copy-law, present tense:** "Before you install anything" (`index.astro:95`) presupposes an install path, contradicting the hero 1,700px above. "…become part of what every listener sees" (`:126-128`) implies a shipped user base. Both want future tense.
- **Unverified product fact:** "Uzume **separates each track into stems**" (`index.astro:30`). Stems/source-separation appears nowhere in `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, or `WEBSITE_PLAN.md`. `index.astro:15` asserts every fact is checked against the app repo — this one needs that check cited, or should retreat to "analyses each track and measures what is in it."
- **`/design` is live, crawlable and un-`noindex`'d**, containing a disabled "Download beta" affordance. The `noindex` prop already exists on `Base.astro:12-14`.
- `strict-transport-security: max-age=0` — HSTS present but disabled on every response.
- `<meta name="generator" content="Astro v7.3.2">` leaks the exact framework version.
- `robots.txt` is Cloudflare's managed content-signals file, not the project's. No `sitemap.xml` (`@astrojs/sitemap` absent).
- **False positives discarded:** the `<a href="/">` wrapping the wordmark image is not an empty link (accessible name "Uzume"); the hardcoded dark canvas is intentional, not a dark-mode smell; `Source on GitHub` at 104.6×16.5 is an inline text link, exempt under WCAG 2.5.8; `/docs` small targets and its `H2`-before-`H1` are Starlight chrome, not repo-authored.

## Questions to Consider

1. **If the footage is the brand, why did the chrome ship first?** Shipping "recede" alone produced exactly the generic dark page the restraint was designed to avoid. Would one 8-second clip — even imperfect, even captured off a display — have been worth more than everything below the fold?
2. **Who is the homepage for?** It serves contributor and listener with equal weight and two identical violet buttons. A Persuade surface can only have one primary. Pick one; give the other a link.
3. **Why does the myth get the closing position instead of the ask?**
4. **Is the Coleridge line a tagline or a mood?** At 40px it is the page's headline and it does not describe the product. Would it be stronger at caption scale beneath a product sentence set in Alumni Sans — signature rather than claim?
5. **Why do the trust banners get to be the most colorful thing on the site?** Blue, olive and green appear nowhere in the opening spectrum, deployed in the one place the design system says color must be earned by content. What if they were tonal surfaces with a violet/gold/cyan rule and a colored icon, keeping the spectrum reserved for the engine?
