# Uzume Brand

> Working notes for BRAND.1. This file remains provisional until the Task-5 direction is chosen.

## Foundations

Uzume is pronounced **oo-ZOO-meh**. The identity principle is **the engine's output is the brand**: a dark, restrained, typographic theater that gives the app's footage the entire color field. The sole chrome accent is First Light amber (`#F2A64A` and state variants); it is never joined by a second accent.

## Direction A — The Crack of Light

The wordmark uses taut, open custom strokes interrupted once at the shoulder of the initial U, where a narrow amber wedge makes the name feel physically opened rather than decorated. The icon reduces the same event to its most durable form: a dark field divided by one precise diagonal seam of first light. This is the quietest and most timeless direction, with the strongest 16 px silhouette and the least dependence on knowing the myth.

## Direction B — The Mirror Catch

The wordmark is rounder and more reflective in character, with a single off-center amber gleam caught inside the final letter rather than a separate emblem. The icon treats reflection abstractly as nested tonal discs and an asymmetrical highlight—never a depicted ritual object—giving it a warmer, more optical presence in the Dock. It is the most sensuous direction and the closest to visualizer language without becoming an eye or lens logo.

## Direction C — The Rhythm

The wordmark is built from repeated vertical measures, including a doubled U and modular joins, so the lettering itself feels timed. One vertical beat lights in amber and becomes five reduced bars in the icon, where differing heights imply a composed phrase rather than a live waveform. This is the most contributor-facing and music-software-literate direction, but it carries the greatest risk of reading as generic audio tooling.
